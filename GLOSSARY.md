# Sleeper

Sleeper finds underpriced copies of trading cards one collector wants. These are the words its
pages, alerts and API use.

## Language

**Want**:
A card the collector is looking for, with the searches that find it and the ceilings set for it.
_Avoid_: Watch, target, wishlist item

**Listing**:
One copy of a wanted card for sale on eBay, as either a graded slab or a raw card.
_Avoid_: Item, result, lot

**Deal**:
A listing under the ceiling that is also worth its price: it beats recent sold prices for the
card, or it is a raw copy worth buying to grade. A card with no sold prices yet counts on its
ceiling alone, except a running auction, which waits until its last six hours.
_Avoid_: Bargain, hit, match

**New deal**:
A deal Sleeper first saw in the last 24 hours. The same on every device: it does not depend on
what the collector has already looked at.
_Avoid_: Unseen, unread, recent

**Landed price**:
What a listing costs delivered to the forwarding address: its price plus delivery.
_Avoid_: Total, sticker price, cost

**Ceiling**:
The most the collector will pay, landed, for a want in a given condition.
_Avoid_: Cap, limit, budget, max price

**Max bid**:
The highest bid on an auction that still leaves it a deal.
_Avoid_: Limit, snipe amount

**Snipe**:
A bid placed for the collector in an auction's last seconds by Gixen, at the max bid. The
collector adds each one; Sleeper never places it.
_Avoid_: Autobid, proxy bid

**Block**:
The collector's decision to take a listing off the live lists and never be alerted to it again.
A blocked listing that had already ended stays on the Ended page.
_Avoid_: Hide, dismiss, ignore

**Sweep**:
One pass over eBay for every want.
_Avoid_: Scan, poll, crawl

**Ended deal**:
A deal or alerted listing that eBay has since closed, sold or unsold.
_Avoid_: Expired, closed, past deal
